\chapter{Localization maps are weakly étale}
\label{ch:mathlib-weakly-etale-localization}

This chapter backs the infrastructure file
\texttt{Proetale/Mathlib/RingTheory/WeaklyEtale/Localization.lean}. The
single statement it carries is item (1) of Stacks \stacksproject{092J}:
a localization $R \to S^{-1} R$ at a multiplicative submonoid is weakly
étale. The substantive informal proof currently lives in the sibling
chapter \texttt{Mathlib\_RingTheory\_WeaklyEtale\_Subalgebra.tex},
\Cref{lem:weaklyEtale-localization}; the plan agent should migrate the
prose to this chapter in a future iteration.

\section{Statement}

\begin{lemma}\leanok
[\stacksproject{092J}, localization clause]
  \label{lem:mathlib-weaklyEtale-localization-mirror}
  \lean{Algebra.WeaklyEtale.of_isLocalization}
  \uses{def:weakly-etale-algebra}
  Let $R$ be a commutative ring, $S \subseteq R$ a multiplicative
  submonoid, and $T$ a localization of $R$ at $S$. Then the structure
  map $R \to T$ is weakly étale.
\end{lemma}

The Lean rendering exposes the same content as a derived instance
\lean{Algebra.WeaklyEtale.localization} for the canonical Mathlib
construction \texttt{Localization S}; the standalone theorem
\texttt{of\_isLocalization} above is the variant that takes $T$ and the
\texttt{IsLocalization} witness as inputs (so the explicit submonoid
needn't be inferable from \texttt{WeaklyEtale R T}).

\section{Proof sketch}

Unwinding Mathlib's definition of \texttt{Algebra.WeaklyEtale}~$R\,T$,
the obligation is two flatness conditions:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item[(F1)] $T$ is flat over $R$ — discharged by
    \texttt{IsLocalization.flat}.
  \item[(F2)] The multiplication map
    $\mathrm{lmul}'_R : T \otimes_R T \to T$ is flat — that is,
    $T \otimes_R T$ is flat over $T$ via this map. This step uses the
    canonical $T$-algebra isomorphism
    $(S^{-1} R) \otimes_R (S^{-1} R) \cong S^{-1}(S^{-1} R)$ and a
    second application of \texttt{IsLocalization.flat}, this time with
    base ring $S^{-1} R$.
\end{enumerate}

(F1) is closed in the present iteration. (F2) is recorded as a
\texttt{sorry} pending iteration $\geq 136$.
