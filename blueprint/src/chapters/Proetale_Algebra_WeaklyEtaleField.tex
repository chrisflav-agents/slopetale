\chapter{Weakly étale algebras over a field}
\label{ch:proetale-algebra-weaklyEtaleField}

This chapter is a placeholder for the per-file blueprint of
\texttt{Proetale/Algebra/WeaklyEtaleField.lean}. The substantive informal
material currently lives in \texttt{more-on-local-structure.tex}
(Sections \ref{lem:flat-tensor-over-field-imples-algebraic},
\ref{lem:isAlgebraic-of-weaklyEtale}, and
\ref{thm:weakly-etale-imples-ind-etale-over-fields}) and should be migrated
or summarised here in a future iteration. The Lean file collects the three
results:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \texttt{Algebra.WeaklyEtale.isAlgebraic} (Stacks 092P): a weakly
        étale extension of fields is separable algebraic.
  \item \texttt{Algebra.WeaklyEtale.isAlgebraic\_of\_weaklyEtale}
        (Stacks 092Q (2) / 0CKR(3)): every element of a weakly étale
        algebra over a field is algebraic. iter-143: closed modulo the
        residual sorry on \texttt{isAlgebraic}, via the per-prime route
        \texttt{Algebra.WeaklyEtale.of\_isLocalization} (Lane M closure
        iter-142) + \texttt{Algebra.WeaklyEtale.trans} + the iso
        \texttt{Localization.AtPrime} is a field for absolutely flat $R$.
  \item \texttt{Algebra.WeaklyEtale.etale\_of\_fg}: every finitely generated
        $K$-subalgebra of a weakly étale $K$-algebra (over a field) is étale.
  \item \texttt{Algebra.WeaklyEtale.indEtale\_field} (Stacks 092Q (1)):
        a weakly étale algebra over a field is ind-étale.
\end{itemize}
