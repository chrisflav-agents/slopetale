## Why this source

iter-147 directive `purity-flat-literature` asks whether the **specialized cycle-breaker** for the L195 sub-sorry in `Proetale/Algebra/WeaklyEtaleField.lean` is provable from the literature. The candidate statement is:

> Let `K` be a field and `K → L` weakly étale, with `L' = K(a') ⊆ L` a finitely generated `K`-subfield. Then `K → L'` is weakly étale.

Section 10.108 supplies the algebra-side definition and 11-way characterization of pure ideals (so weakly étale = `mult: L⊗L → L` has pure kernel can be unfolded). Section 15.107 supplies the immediate functoriality consequences over a field — Lemma 0CKR(3) explicitly states *any fg K-subalgebra of a weakly étale K-algebra is a finite product of finite separable extensions of K*, and Lemma 0CKT(2) gives `B_max(A') = B_max(A) ∩ A'` for `A' ⊂ A`. These together resolve the directive's "specialized cycle-breaker" via direct citation, without needing pure-ideal descent through the inclusion `L' ⊗_K L' ↪ L ⊗_K L`.

### More on Algebra §15.107 "Weakly étale algebras over fields" (tag 0CKQ) — lines 313-587

- `\begin{lemma}` `\label{lemma-class-weakly-etale-over-field}` — **Lemma 15.107.1 (tag 0CKR)** — 6-part property bundle for weakly étale `K → B` (`K` a field): reduced; integral over `K`; *any fg K-subalgebra is a finite product of finite separable extensions of K*; field-iff-no-nontrivial-idempotents; sub/quotient preserves weakly étale; tensor product stays weakly étale.   ← **this is the lemma that resolves the directive's specialized cycle-breaker.**
- `\begin{lemma}` `\label{lemma-max-weakly-etale-subalgebra}` — **Lemma 15.107.2 (tag 0CKS)** — every `K`-algebra `A` has a maximal weakly étale `K`-subalgebra `B_max ⊂ A`.
- `\begin{lemma}` `\label{lemma-properties-of-max-weakly-etale-subalgebra}` — **Lemma 15.107.3 (tag 0CKT)** — functoriality of `B_max`: morphisms, sub-intersection (`B_max(A') = B_max(A) ∩ A'`), filtered colimits, nilpotents, products, no-idempotents-implies-field-and-separable-algebraic.
- `\begin{lemma}` `\label{lemma-change-fields-max-weakly-etale-subalgebra}` — **Lemma 15.107.4 (tag 0CKU)** — `B_max` commutes with base change `K → L` along arbitrary field extensions.

Similarly §15.107 cites lemmas from §15.106 already vendored verbatim in `stacks-092PQ.tex`.
- **Iter-147 directive note.** The directive's framing — "descent of pure ideals from `L ⊗_K L` to `L' ⊗_K L'`" — turns out NOT to be the cleanest route: the §15.107 toolkit goes through the over-a-field characterization (`L = colim étale subalgebras`), not pure-ideal contraction. Stacks Project does not contain a standalone lemma stating "pure ideals contract to pure ideals along flat injections of rings"; what it does contain (10.108) is enough to derive contraction by hand under the specific hypotheses of the question, but the planner should prefer the §15.107 path.
